Mermaze
Description
Mermaze is a game jam project inspired by the theme of water. You take on the role of King Neptune, controlling the tides and winds to guide your mermaid children safely home. Navigate through a maze filled with obstacles and enemies, using strategic adjustments to help them reach their destination.

Features
🌊 Tide & Wind Mechanics – Control the ocean’s forces to alter the movement of your mermaid children.
🧜‍♀️ Maze Navigation – Overcome obstacles and avoid dangerous enemies.
⏳ Time-Based Strategy – Plan your movements wisely to ensure their safe passage.
🎮 Game Jam Creation – Developed under time constraints for a creative challenge.

How to Play
Control the Tides & Winds – Adjust the environmental forces using in-game controls.
Guide the Mermaids – Navigate them through the treacherous maze.
Avoid Dangers – Keep them safe from enemies and obstacles.
Reach the Goal – Ensure all mermaids make it home safely!
